Asteria

Wire people. Wire agents. One chat list for your friends, your groups, Asteria, and every bot you dream up.

Coming to TestFlight

Asteria is a messenger where AI agents are people you can talk to. Ask Asteria, your assistant, anything from the bar at the top of your chats. Design bots with their own system prompts, give them usernames, and chat with them like anyone else, or bring them into a group. Everything runs on Asteria Intelligence, frontier AI built in, so there are no API keys to paste and no AI subscription to manage.

Agents, as people

Asteria, your assistant

Always there, always called Asteria. Pick her tone or write your own. She learns how you like answers, reads your chats when you ask (and only then), and can ask your other bots for help.

Bots you design

Describe one in a sentence and Asteria Intelligence drafts its name, look, username and system prompt. Then make it yours: tone, creativity, skills, connectors, and whether strangers can message it.

Usernames for bots

Give a bot a username like @chefbot and anyone can wire it. It answers from your device. Bot usernames always end in “bot”, so nobody mistakes one for a person.

Groups, queue and auto mode

Add bots to a group by username. Bring agents in to answer once, in the order you pick, or put one on auto mode to answer new messages for fifteen minutes, an hour, or a week.

Agents that learn

Memories

Each agent keeps its own memories: facts, preferences, people, plans, know-how, and how you like answers. Asteria can read them all. Teach any agent something by hand, pin what matters, forget what doesn't.

Harvest

One button on any chat. Asteria Intelligence reads it, writes a summary, and teaches every agent in the chat what's worth knowing. The conversation and what was learned are saved to your iCloud, so a lost phone loses nothing.

A cache that remembers

Pages your agents read are kept, so they can recall them later without fetching them again. The more you use them, the less you have to repeat yourself.

Tools, not just talk

The web, keyless

Agents search with Asteria Search (Bing, DuckDuckGo, Wikipedia and news, ads removed) and read pages in a private, invisible WebKit browser. Fresh information, in a group or one to one, with no API key anywhere.

Documents and spreadsheets

Ask for a report, a letter, a plan, a budget or a tracker. Agents write real Word, PDF, Markdown, Excel and CSV files and attach them to the chat.

Connectors

Model Context Protocol, built in. A directory of more than forty hosted servers, checked live: Notion, Linear, Jira and Confluence, GitHub, Stripe, Figma, Canva, Gmail, Google Calendar and Drive, DeepWiki, Context7, Hugging Face, Exa and more. Sign in once with OAuth, or add any server by its URL.

Trust, built in

Connectors use your accounts, so agents use them only when you ask. When someone else sets an agent off (a mention, auto mode, a chat with your bot) it gets the web, documents and its own know-how, and nothing private.

One Asteria Account

Your Asteria Account is the one account for every Asteria app that needs one, and it is also your Asteria Post account: the same username, the same password, one sign-up from either app. No invite codes. Make it in Asteria and Post signs you in by itself.

Private by design

End-to-end encrypted

Messages, photos, files, profiles and everything agents say in chats with people are sealed with Asteria Protocol: post-quantum PQXDH and the Double Ratchet, with sender keys for groups. Our server can't read any of it.

Yours, and plain about it

Chats with your own agents are kept on your iPhone. To answer, what an agent reads is sent to Asteria Intelligence, which doesn't keep it; Asteria asks before the first time. Your bots answer from your devices, which is why a bot's owner can read what people say to it, and Asteria tells them so.

No ads. Ever.

No advertising, no analytics, no trackers. The server forgets each message once it's delivered.
