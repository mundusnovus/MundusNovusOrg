Your account

Do I need an invite?

No. Download Asteria and make an Asteria Account: a name, a username, your email and a password.

Is my Asteria Account the same as my Asteria Post account?

Yes. One account, one username, one password. Sign up in either app and the other works too. If you already have an Asteria Post account, sign in to Asteria with it.

How do I delete my account?

You › Delete Asteria Account, then type your password. It deletes your Asteria Account, your Asteria Post account and your bots' usernames.

Asteria and your bots

Why can't I use agents?

Agents run on Apple Intelligence, so they need an iPhone or iPad that supports it, with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings. If it's still downloading, they'll be ready shortly.

How do I make a bot?

Tap + New bot above your chats (or in Agents). Describe it in a sentence and tap Design it with Apple Intelligence, or fill it in yourself. Give it a username ending in “bot” if you want other people to be able to message it.

How do I add a bot to a group?

Open the group, tap its name, then Add a bot, and type its username. A bot runs on its owner's devices, so it can join groups its owner is in.

What are queue and auto mode?

In any chat, tap ⋯ › Bring in an agent. Pick agents to answer once, in order, or turn on auto mode so one answers new messages by itself for the time you choose.

Why didn't my bot answer someone?

Bots answer from your device, so Asteria has to be open (or recently open) on your iPhone or iPad. Check that the bot has “Anyone can message it” on.

What does Harvest do?

It saves the conversation to your iCloud and teaches the agents in it what's worth remembering. Open a chat, tap ⋯, then Harvest. See everything they've learned in Agents › Memories.

Connectors

How do I connect Notion, GitHub or another service?

Agents › Connectors. Open servers work straight away; others sign you in with the service. You can also add any MCP server by its URL.

Why does Gmail ask for a client ID?

Google's Workspace MCP servers are in Developer Preview and don't let apps register themselves. Create an OAuth client of type iOS in Google Cloud with the bundle ID technology.asteria.wire and paste its client ID.

Privacy and safety

Can Asteria read my messages?

No. They're end-to-end encrypted with Asteria Protocol. Only the people (and, for bots, the bot's owner) in a chat can read it.

What's a safety number?

Each one-to-one chat with a person has a 60-digit safety number. Compare it in person or on a call. If yours match, no one is in the middle. Open the chat, tap the name, then Safety number.

How do I block someone?

Open the chat, tap the name, and tap Block.
