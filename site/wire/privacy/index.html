Privacy Policy

Asteria

Effective 5 November 2026.

The short version: your messages are end-to-end encrypted and Asteria Technology can't read them. Your agents answer with Asteria Intelligence: what they read to answer you goes through our gateway, which keeps none of it, to our AI provider, DeepSeek, in China. What they learn is kept in your own iCloud. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Your Asteria Account

Your Asteria Account is an account on Asteria Post (post.asteria.technology), our server on the AT Protocol network. To make one we keep your username (also your Post handle), your display name, your email address (for account recovery and sign-in codes) and your password (stored by the server as a hash). Your Post profile and posts are public on the AT Protocol network, as they are in any app that uses it. Asteria's message server never receives your password: the app signs in to Asteria Post directly and shows our message server only a short-lived proof.

Messages

Messages, photos, videos, voice messages, files, stickers, reactions, edits, read receipts, group details and profiles are encrypted end to end with Asteria Protocol (post-quantum PQXDH and the Double Ratchet, with sender keys for groups). The keys that open them never leave your devices.

To deliver messages our server keeps: your username and display name (so people can find you by username), your public keys, which account a message is for and from, until it is picked up, and, if you turn on notifications, your device's push token and your notification settings. Encrypted messages are deleted when they're delivered, or after 30 days. Encrypted attachments are deleted after 30 days. We don't log IP addresses with messages, and we keep no record of who you talk to after a message is delivered.

Agents and Asteria Intelligence

Asteria (your assistant) and the bots you make answer with Asteria Intelligence. The first time an agent is asked anything, Asteria asks whether to turn it on, and you can turn it off in You › Asteria Intelligence. Chats with your own agents are stored only on your device. To answer, an agent sends what it needs to Asteria Intelligence: the agent's instructions and memories, your request, recent messages in the chat it's answering in (including other people's messages in a group where you brought it in or mentioned it), photos you show it, and the results of tools it uses (web pages, your connectors, your chats when you ask it to read them). You can keep any chat out of Asteria's reach in that chat's info.

Requests go from your device to Asteria Technology's gateway (asteria.technology/intelligence), which passes them to our AI provider and streams the answer back. The gateway doesn't store or log what's sent or answered; it keeps only how much your account used each day, under a label that can't be traced back to you, for 40 days. Our AI provider is DeepSeek (Hangzhou DeepSeek Artificial Intelligence Co., Ltd.), which processes and stores requests in the People's Republic of China under its own privacy policy and terms, and may keep them or use them to improve its services. It receives an unlinkable label, not your name, username or account. Leave out anything you wouldn't want stored.

A bot's name, username, one-line description and look are stored on our server so people can find it. A bot answers from its owner's devices: if you message someone's bot, the message is encrypted to its owner, its owner can read it, and to answer, the owner's device sends it to Asteria Intelligence. Asteria shows this in every chat with someone else's bot.

The web

When an agent searches the web, your device sends the search words directly to Bing, DuckDuckGo and Wikipedia (through Asteria Search). When it reads a page, your device loads it in a private WebKit view with no cookies kept. Those sites handle the requests under their own policies. Asteria Technology doesn't receive them.

Connectors

If you connect a service (for example Notion, GitHub or Gmail) through the Model Context Protocol, your device talks to that service directly, and its sign-in is stored in your iCloud Keychain. Agents use your connectors only when you ask them something, never when someone else sets them off. The service handles your data under its own policy.

iCloud

Your agents, their memories, harvested conversations, pages they read and your connectors' settings are stored in your iCloud account's private database, which only you can read. Asteria Technology can't see it. You can erase it all in the app (You › Asteria Intelligence).

No ads, no tracking

Asteria has no advertising, no analytics and no third-party trackers. Nothing sent to Asteria Intelligence is used for advertising.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to You › Delete Asteria Account. This deletes your Asteria Account and your Asteria Post account (with its posts), your bots' usernames, and everything our message server holds for you. You can also erase what your agents learned from your iCloud at the same time. Signing out erases your chats and keys from that device.

Children

Asteria is not directed to children under 13.

Changes and contact
