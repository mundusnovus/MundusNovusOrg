Privacy Policy

Asteria Browse

Effective 9 November 2026.

We don't collect your data

  • Your tabs, Spaces, bookmarks, history and downloads list are stored only on your device. Asteria Technology never receives them.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking. An Asteria Account is optional.

Your Asteria Account

You can sign in with your Asteria Account (your username and password, the same as in Smartz and Post), or the app picks up a sign-in from Smartz or Post on the same device. Signed in, your Smartz tokens are your account's, and your chats with Smartz in the Smartz panel are kept on Asteria's server for your account, sealed (encrypted), so they continue in any Asteria app. When you chat with Smartz, it can see the page you're on (not in private tabs) and your open tabs' titles. Signed out, chats stay on this device.

Search

When you search, your device sends the search words directly to Wikipedia, DuckDuckGo and Bing to get results. Those services handle the request under their own privacy policies. The request identifies the app as Asteria Browse; it does not include an account or anything that identifies you to Asteria Technology.

Websites

Pages you open are loaded by Apple's WebKit, directly from those websites, which may use cookies and other data under their own policies. You can see and remove website data in the app. Private tabs keep site data in a temporary store that is discarded when the tab closes.

Camera, microphone and location

A website can use these only after you allow it in the app, and the system asks for your permission first.

Smartz

Sorting results, the overview, page briefs, answers about a page or your tabs, explanations of a selection and Find It Again use Smartz. Nothing is sent until you turn it on, and you can turn it off in Settings › Search. With it on, the app sends what that feature reads: your search and the top results' titles and snippets, the text of the page you asked about, what you selected, or the titles and text of your open tabs.

Requests go from your device to Asteria Technology's gateway (asteria.technology/intelligence), which passes them to our AI provider and streams the answer back. The gateway doesn't store or log what's sent or answered; it keeps only how much this copy of the app used each day, under a label that can't be traced back to you, for 40 days. To keep the service for our apps only, the app proves it is a genuine copy with Apple's App Attest, which contains no personal data. Our AI provider is Google: Smartz answers with Gemini 4 Argon, Google's AI model, through Google's paid Gemini API. Under the Gemini API terms, Google doesn't use what we send it or what it answers to improve its products or train its models. It keeps them for up to 55 days only to detect and prevent abuse, and people at Google may look at them only for that reason; it may process and briefly store them in any country where it or its agents have facilities. It receives no name, username or account.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

Changes and contact
