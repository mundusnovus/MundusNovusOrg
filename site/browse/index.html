Asteria Browse

A browser that minds its own business. Search that does the reading first.

Join the beta on TestFlight

Asteria Browse is a native WebKit browser with Spaces, favorites, pinned tabs and a sidebar that does not need a tour. The search bar runs Asteria Search: the device asks Bing, DuckDuckGo, Wikipedia, news, images and video sources directly, merges what comes back, removes the ads and trackers, and lets Asteria Intelligence put the useful results first.

New in 26.1: Asteria Intelligence beside every page

This Page

A brief of what the page says, questions answered from the page with citations that find the passage, an explanation of the text you selected, and translation of the brief. ⇧⌘I on the Mac, or the sparkle in the iPhone bar.

Your Tabs

One question answered across every open page in the space, with the tab each fact came from. Organize My Tabs sorts them into spaces by purpose, which is more than most of us manage.

Find it again

In History, describe a page: “the ramen place I looked at last week”. Asteria Intelligence turns the sentence into words and a time window, and the history on this device is matched locally.

Siri

“Brief this page in Asteria Browse” reads the front page's brief aloud. “Organize my tabs” opens the panel. It only reads and suggests, never clicks or types on a page, and works in private tabs without keeping anything.

What it does

Asteria Search 2.0

All, Websites, Articles, Images and Videos. Bing, DuckDuckGo, Wikipedia, Bing News, Bing Images, Wikimedia Commons and Bing Videos, merged with reciprocal rank fusion. Brave is available but off by default. Google is never scraped; choose it as your engine if you want it.

Asteria Intelligence, when you want it

Once you turn it on, the top results are judged for usefulness, ads, spam and relevance by Asteria Intelligence. Ads and spam sink or disappear; a reference work is never removed. A short overview is written with numbered sources, because a citation is cheaper than an apology.

Drag it where it goes

Drag a tab into Favorites, Pinned, a bookmark folder or another Space. Drag a search result into the sidebar to save it. Empty sections show where to drop. Every drag also has a menu and a VoiceOver action, because accessibility is not a nice-to-have.

Private tabs

Each private tab gets its own temporary website data, no history, no searches, no downloads list. It does not hide your traffic from the network. It does keep the tab out of everything else, which is the part you asked for.

Siri and Spotlight

Ask Siri to search, rerun a past search, open a bookmark or open a private tab. History, searches and bookmarks can appear in Spotlight. Private tabs never do. Settings has one switch for the whole lot.

Everything else

Find on page, zoom, print and PDF, downloads with resume, website data controls, permission prompts, external links, and a search engine picker that includes Google, Startpage, Kagi, DuckDuckGo, Bing and Brave. Safari tabs, bookmarks and extensions are not synced or loaded.

Asteria Search is not a search engine

It is a library with opinions. It does not crawl the web, run an index, or pretend to know everything. It asks public sources, merges the results, removes ad networks and tracking parameters, and lets Asteria Intelligence re-rank the top twelve. The overview is generated from snippets, so a claim or a citation can be wrong; the sources are shown beside it for that reason. There is no Asteria search server, no account and no API key. Asteria Intelligence reads only what you ask it about, and doesn’t keep it.

Our promise

A proprietary Asteria Technology app. No ads, ever. We price most apps upfront and keep in-app purchases to a minimum, for upgrades only. The browser is built on Apple’s WebKit. The rest of the personality is ours.

Asteria Intelligence asks before it turns on, and Settings has the switch. Search itself still asks the sources from your device.
