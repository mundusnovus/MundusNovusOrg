Privacy Policy

Asteria Food and Mood, for iPhone

Effective 9 November 2026.

Asteria Food and Mood is a journal app made by Asteria Technology. This policy says what happens to what you write in it.

We don't collect your data

  • Your journal is saved on your iPhone and in your own iCloud account, in a private database only you can reach. Apple stores it for you under Apple's privacy policy. Asteria Technology can't see it.
  • Your name and water goal are kept in your iCloud too, so your other devices have them.
  • There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

The AI

The app's AI features use Smartz. Nothing is sent until you turn it on, and you can turn it off in Settings. With it on, the app sends what that feature needs: what you say about a meal, a meal photo you take or pick (it isn't kept on your iPhone either), or, for a summary, visit questions or an answer, a shortened copy of the days of your journal it reads. That can include health information such as mood, energy, digestion, supplements and medicines, so share only what you're comfortable sending.

Requests go from your device to Asteria Technology's gateway (asteria.technology/intelligence), which passes them to our AI provider and streams the answer back. The gateway doesn't store or log what's sent or answered; it keeps only how much this copy of the app used each day, under a label that can't be traced back to you, for 40 days. To keep the service for our apps only, the app proves it is a genuine copy with Apple's App Attest, which contains no personal data. Our AI provider is Google: Smartz answers with Gemini 4 Argon, Google's AI model, through Google's paid Gemini API. Under the Gemini API terms, Google doesn't use what we send it or what it answers to improve its products or train its models. It keeps them for up to 55 days only to detect and prevent abuse, and people at Google may look at them only for that reason; it may process and briefly store them in any country where it or its agents have facilities. It receives no name, username or account.

The coach, Smartz, and your Asteria Account

The Coach tab has two chats: Food and Mood's coach and Smartz. Both read the last 30 days of your journal and sleep log while you chat, and both go to Smartz like the features above. An Asteria Account is optional. Signed in, your chats with them are kept on Asteria's server for your account, sealed (encrypted), so they continue in any Asteria app on any device; delete a chat in the app to delete it everywhere. They may include health information you or they mention. Signed out, chats stay on this iPhone.

Your voice

Record Your Meal and photo notes show your words as you speak with Apple's speech recognition, on your iPhone. When you finish, the recording goes through Asteria's gateway to ElevenLabs, which writes down what you said and handles the recording under its privacy policy, without your name or account. Talking to the coach or Smartz, and having answers read aloud, work the same way. The audio isn't kept by the app or our gateway. The words are then used like typed ones: nothing is saved until you confirm it.

Sharing

When you export your journal (Excel, PDF or CSV), the file is made on your iPhone. You choose where it goes, for example to your nutritionist. Once you send it, the person you send it to holds that copy.

Deleting your journal

Delete moments in the app. To remove everything, delete the app. Then, in Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage, delete Food and Mood's data.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at asteria.technology/foodandmood/privacy with a new date. Questions: asteria.technology.
