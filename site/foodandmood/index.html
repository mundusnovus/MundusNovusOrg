Food and Mood

The paper journal your nutritionist gave you. Without the paper, the smudges, or the “what did I eat on Tuesday”.

Food and Mood is a journal for what you eat and drink, how you feel, how you move and how your digestion goes. It follows the six-column paper journal many nutritionists hand out: foods, drinks, supplements, energy and mood, movement, and digestion. Then it turns your days into a spreadsheet that opens cleanly in Excel, and a PDF that does not look like a receipt from 2004.

What it does

Write as the day goes

Foods, drinks, supplements, energy and mood, movement, and digestion. Wake time, bedtime, a reflection, mood and energy from 1 to 5, Bristol type and water. Delete a meal, or set its day and time after the fact.

Say it in your own words

Describe breakfast, lunch and the evening. Apple Intelligence keeps them as separate meals, with the times you gave, and leaves sleep and the day’s notes where they belong. You check everything before it is saved, which is the only sensible arrangement.

Siri, briefly

“Log water in Food and Mood.” “How’s my day in Food and Mood.” Ask the journal a question about the last month, find a meal in Spotlight, or export the journal to Shortcuts. Answers describe what you wrote; they are not medical advice.

The spreadsheet is the point

Choose the days and send an Excel workbook with an overview, every moment in one sortable table, a summary of each day, and a page for each day laid out like the paper journal. PDF and CSV are there too.

Bring in the old journal

Import the original Food & Mood Journal workbook. Times are put in order, Bristol types and water are picked out of your notes, and every word you wrote is kept. Importing the same file twice does not double anything.

iCloud, privately

The journal is stored with SwiftData in your own private iCloud database, so it moves to a new iPhone with you. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Asteria cannot read it. Neither can anyone else who asks nicely.

What’s new in 2.5

A new icon, single-day exports that no longer include the day before, Siri water logging and day summaries, an Ask Your Journal box on Patterns, and a clearer iCloud status in Settings. The journal is still yours. The paper is still gone.
