A social app on the open AT Protocol network, with Asteria Intelligence building your feed. Outrage sinks. Promotion sinks. When you reach the end, it tells you you're all caught up. Imagine.

Asteria Browse · Mac, iPhone and iPad

A browser that minds its own business.

Native WebKit, Spaces, and a sidebar that doesn't need a guided tour. Asteria Intelligence sits beside every page, reads it when you ask, and never clicks or types a thing for you. Restraint: our most requested feature.

  • Asteria Intelligence beside every pageA brief of the page, answers with citations that find the passage, and an explanation of whatever you selected. ⇧⌘I on the Mac.
  • Your tabs, sortedAsk one question across every open tab, or let Organize My Tabs sort them into spaces by purpose.
  • Find it againDescribe the page you half remember. History is matched on this device, not on ours. We don't have one.
  • Drag it where it goesTabs into Favorites, Pinned, a folder or another space. Every drag also has a menu and a VoiceOver action.
  • Private tabs that mean itEach gets its own temporary website data and keeps no history, searches or downloads.

Asteria Search · the engine inside Browse

Not a search engine. A search with opinions.

Asteria Search doesn't crawl the web or pretend to know everything. It asks the sources directly from your device, throws out the ads, and has Asteria Intelligence tell the rest to form an orderly queue.

Google is never scraped. Choose it as Browse's search engine if you like; that's a different sentence. There's no Asteria search server, no account, no API key and no query history.

  • Inside Browse 26.1
  • All, Websites, Articles, Images, Videos
  • No query history
How Search works Try it in the Browse beta
  1. Ask the sourcesStraight from your device, each one asked directly.
    BingDuckDuckGoWikipediaBing NewsBing ImagesWikimedia CommonsBing Videos
  2. Bin the adsAd networks, ad-click redirects and paid-campaign links are dropped. Tracking keys go too.
  3. Merge fairlyReciprocal rank fusion merges the lists. Whichever source answers first gets no prize.
  4. Let the model sortAsteria Intelligence judges the top 12: useful, advertising, spam or off topic. Reference works are never removed.
  5. Write the overviewTwo to four sentences with numbered sources. It reads snippets, so it can be wrong, which is why the sources sit right beside it.

Asteria Food and Mood · iPhone

The food journal, minus the paper.

The six-column journal your nutritionist hands out, rebuilt in Swift. Say what you ate in your own words, check it, and send a spreadsheet that opens cleanly in Excel. Your nutritionist may weep with joy. Results vary.

  • Say it in your own wordsAsteria Intelligence keeps each meal separate, with the time you gave. You check everything before it's saved.
  • Snap a mealAsteria Intelligence writes down the foods and drinks it can see. The photo isn't kept. Your lunch's dignity is.
  • Patterns, not diagnosesMood, energy, water, sleep and Bristol types over 7, 14 or 30 days, and what tends to go together, in plain counts. Never “because”.
  • The spreadsheet is the pointExcel, PDF or CSV for any range of days, with a page for each day laid out like the paper journal.
  • Private by designStored in your own iCloud. No accounts, no ads, no tracking. We can't read your journal, and frankly we don't want to.

    How we work

    The fine print, in large print.

    Short version: we make the app, you pay for it once, and then we leave you alone.

    No ads. Ever.

    Not in Post, not in Browse, not anywhere. We'd rather charge you once and go back to work. It's less a moral position than a scheduling one.

    Upfront by default.

    Prices are on the page, not behind a paywall in chapter three. In-app purchases are upgrades, not hostage situations. If an app is free, it's because it's free.

    Asteria Intelligence, on purpose.

    Frontier AI in every app. It ranks, summarises and suggests, tells you when it wrote something, and asks before it starts. Siri and Writing Tools stay Apple’s, on your device.

    Siri, with a job.

    Catch me up. Brief this page. Log water. Clock out. Siri does the short, useful things. It still can't fix your printer. Nobody can.

    iCloud, not our cloud.

    Your journal, vault, shifts and history live in your private iCloud. No analytics dashboard, no growth team. There's barely a team.

    Built in Swift.

    Native frameworks, SF Pro, Liquid Glass, and motion that knows when to stop. The apps are proprietary; the web tools and the course are open.
