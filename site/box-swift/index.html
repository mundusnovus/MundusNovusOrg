Asteria Box native

The music maker, rebuilt in Swift. The model writes the arrangement. You still take the credit.

Box has been a browser instrument for years: patterns, a real band, a mix that balances itself and MP3s ready to share. The native 2.0 is the same idea with Apple’s frameworks underneath, a proper document model, and an assistant that writes notes instead of excuses. It is in development. The browser version is available now and has been for longer than some of our competitors have existed.

What the native one does

Foundation Models composer

Describe a song and get a plan: style, tempo, key, four chords, contour, feel, density, drums and three instruments. The on-device model streams the plan; the Arranger writes every note. The model does not get to play live and call it a day.

Ask for a change

“Faster.” “Add drums.” “Make the melody a piano.” Ask reads the words, proposes a change, clamps it to something musical, and applies it with one Undo. Explicit words win. The model does not get to rearrange the whole song because you said “warmer”.

Patterns, not a piano roll

Each track has numbered one-bar patterns and a sequence. The pattern palette edits the selected bar, so every bar using it changes. New, copy, steal from another track, make silent, empty. Digits and arrow keys work on a keyboard, because this is a Mac app too.

Siri and the audio domain

Song, artist and album entities, play, stop, compose, change, choose an instrument, clean up and delete. An interactive result card with Play, Stop, Open and Undo. Now Playing and Relevant Entities. Siri can play your song; it cannot make it good, but neither can most producers.

iCloud Drive

Songs are files, saved through coordinated reads and writes with debounced saves and conflict copies. The library syncs through your own iCloud Drive. Settings sync through key-value storage. No Asteria account, no Asteria audio server.

Private Cloud Compute, gated

When opted in, entitled and available, the composer can use Apple’s Private Cloud Compute and fall back to the device. Until Apple grants the managed entitlement, the key stays false and everything runs locally. We are not going to ship a switch that does nothing and call it a feature.

Meanwhile, the web Box

Chiptune or a whole band: guitars, bass, organ, strings, choir and a real drum kit. Power-chord riffs, a mix that balances itself, MP3s ready to share, and every song on the soundtrack was written with it. It runs in the browser, installs as an app, and does not ask for an account.
