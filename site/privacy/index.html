Privacy · Effective 7 November 2026

One kit. Nothing extra kept.

Asteria's apps work as one system: one Asteria Account, one Smartz (the AI in every app), and chats that follow you from app to app. This page says exactly what each part keeps, what passes through our server, and what our AI provider sees, because “we don't collect your data” has been used to mean several things, and we would like to be specific.

The short version

  • One Asteria Account for every app, optional where an app works without one. No Asteria analytics. No advertising. No tracking SDKs.
  • Your work lives on your device and, where the app supports it, in your own private iCloud database.
  • Smartz, the AI in Smartz, Post, Browse, Search, Food and Mood, Box, Timeclock and Swift: Zero to Hero and at smartz.chat, asks before anything is sent. Our gateway keeps none of what you send. Our AI provider, DeepInfra, answers in the United States and keeps nothing; Smartz's voice and listening are ElevenLabs, under its own privacy policy. Keys uses Apple Intelligence on your device.
  • We cannot read your vault, journal, songs or search history, or the shifts in your iCloud. Shifts you sync through your Asteria Account are sealed and read back only to you. What you send to Smartz passes through our gateway without being kept. Smartz learns about you only from chats you harvest, keeps it sealed and private, and shows you all of it.

Where each app keeps its data

Smartz

Messages with people and bots are end-to-end encrypted with Asteria Protocol; our server can't read them and deletes each one once it's delivered. Your bots and what they learn live in your private CloudKit database. Smartz's own chats, memories and wiki are on your Asteria Account (below). The app has its own privacy policy.

Browse

Tabs, Spaces, bookmarks, history and downloads stay on your device. Search requests go from the device to the public sources you asked for. Private tabs use temporary website data that is discarded when the tab closes. Siri and Spotlight indexing is opt-in and can be removed from Settings. With Smartz on, what it reads for you (a search's results, a page, a selection, your open tabs) is sent to it.

Keys

Your vault is encrypted on your device with a key wrapped by your master password. Only ciphertext is stored in your private CloudKit database. Your master password and recovery key are never sent anywhere. Apple Intelligence sees counts of findings for the security coach, not passwords, usernames, sites or notes.

Timeclock

Clients, tags, shifts, breaks, rates and settings are stored with SwiftData in your private CloudKit database and, if you sign in with Sync on, on your Asteria Account, sealed (below). The Apple Watch app talks to your iPhone directly. With Smartz on, what you ask it about is sent to it: a sentence for a shift draft, a period's shifts for a summary, or your recent hours for its chat. Calendar access is optional and read-only. Siri and Spotlight sharing are opt-in. Exports are files you choose to create. The app has its own privacy policy.

Food and Mood

The journal is stored with SwiftData in your private CloudKit database. With Smartz on, what you ask it about (a meal you typed or said, a meal photo and its note, or a shortened journal, sleep included, for a summary, an answer or a chat with the coach or Smartz) is sent to it, and nothing is saved until you confirm it. Record Your Meal shows your words with Apple's speech recognition on the iPhone, then Smartz writes down what you said (Smartz's voice, below); the audio isn't stored. Exports are files you choose to create. Food and Mood is not medical advice.

Swift: Zero to Hero

Lessons, code, checklist ticks, streaks and badges are stored in your private CloudKit database. Accent colour and code size use iCloud key-value storage. The on-device exercise engine checks your code without sending it anywhere, and the Mac uses your own Swift compiler. With Smartz on, what you ask it about is sent to it: up to 2,000 characters of your code for the coach or a review, a lesson's title and description for a recap or a mini-lesson, this week's lesson titles for your study plan, and your questions to Smartz.

Box

The native Box stores songs in your iCloud Drive and settings in key-value storage. The web Box stores songs locally or in Asteria Cloud if you choose to use it. Neither asks for an Asteria account to make music on your own device. With Smartz on in the native Box, what you type (a song to compose, a change, a question) is sent to it, with the open song's title and a summary of its music: key, tempo, chords, and each track's instrument and range.

Asteria Learn

Progress is stored in your browser or, if you sign in, in your Asteria account. The free course does not require an account. Lesson code is checked by the course runner; it is not used for advertising or profiling.

Asteria Search

The library has no query history, analytics or cache. It asks public sources directly. Those sources receive the query, your IP address and request metadata under their own policies. Asking from your device does not make search anonymous.

Smartz

Smartz, by Asteria, is the AI in Smartz, Post, Browse, Search, Food and Mood, Box, Timeclock and Swift: Zero to Hero, and at smartz.chat. (Until version 27.2.5 it was called Asteria Intelligence, and the Smartz app was called Asteria.) Nothing is sent until you turn it on in the app, which asks you first. You can turn it off in each app's Settings.

When you use it, what you ask and what you share with it (for example a chat, a page, a post, a photo, part of your journal, a song or your code) goes from your device to Asteria Technology's gateway at asteria.technology/intelligence (or smartz.chat on the web). The gateway passes it to our AI provider and streams the answer back. It doesn't store or log what you send or what comes back, except the chats and memories described below that you choose to keep. It keeps how much was used each day, under a label that can't be traced back to you, for 40 days, so everyone stays within a daily allowance.

Our AI provider is DeepInfra, Inc., which runs the model on its own servers in the United States. DeepInfra doesn't keep what we send it or what it answers, and doesn't use it for training, under its privacy policy. It receives no name, username or account.

To keep the service for our apps only, apps without an account prove they are a genuine copy with Apple's App Attest, which contains no personal data. Apps with an Asteria Account start a session with it, so your daily allowance is the same in every app. At smartz.chat without signing in, the daily allowance is counted per network address, and nothing you type is kept on our server.

Smartz's voice

When you have Smartz read an answer aloud, talk with it in audio mode, dictate a message, or say a meal in Food and Mood, the words to speak and the recording of what you said go through our gateway to ElevenLabs, Inc., which turns text into speech and speech into text, under its privacy policy. ElevenLabs receives no name, username or account with them, and handles them under its own policy, which may keep them for a time. Our gateway keeps neither the audio nor the words. While you speak, Apple's speech recognition shows your words on screen, on your device. Spoken answers are cached on your device so a replay doesn't fetch them again.

What Smartz learns, your wiki, and unified intelligence

Smartz learns about you only when you harvest a chat. Then the chat's text goes to Smartz, which writes down what's worth remembering (memories) and adds to pages about what you've discussed (your wiki). These are kept on Asteria's server for your Asteria Account, sealed (encrypted) before they're stored, so every Asteria app and smartz.chat can use them. When you talk to Smartz yourself, the memories that fit are added to your question; they're never used when someone else sets Smartz off. You can read, edit and delete every memory and page, in any app's What Smartz Knows and in the Smartz app's Wiki tab.

Your wiki is private until you publish it. Published, it's public at smartz.chat/your-username for anyone with the link, until you turn publishing off.

Unified intelligence is off unless you turn it on. With it on, a harvest may also write a general skill, like a good way to plan a trip, into a shared library that makes Smartz better for everyone. Skills are written to carry nothing personal, emails, phone numbers, links and handles are removed before they're stored, and a skill is used for everyone only once several different people's harvests have produced it.

Your Asteria Account, Smartz tokens and chats

Every Asteria app can sign in to an Asteria Account: your username and password, the same as your Asteria Post account. It's optional in Browse, Box, Food and Mood, Timeclock and Swift: Zero to Hero. You can also sign in at smartz.chat (an account can't be made there). Signing in once in Smartz or Post signs in those apps on the same device, each with a sign-in of its own kept in the device's Keychain.

Smartz is measured in Smartz tokens, a daily allowance per account (or per device without one), shown in each app; answers, speaking and listening all count against it, and every answer shows what it cost. The Smartz Subscription's plans will raise the allowance; during the beta every account is on Dedicated, free. We keep how many tokens each account used each day, under a label that can't be traced back to you, for 40 days.

When you're signed in, your chats with Smartz (and with Food and Mood's coach) are kept on Asteria's server for your account, so a chat started in one app continues in any other, on any device, and at smartz.chat. They're sealed (encrypted) before they're stored, and only read back to you. Deleting a chat in any app deletes it everywhere; deleting your Asteria Account deletes them all, with everything Smartz learned about you. Signed out, chats stay on the device. Asteria Technology doesn't read your chats, use them for ads, or sell them; to answer, the words you send in a chat go to Smartz like any other request.

An app can also sync your work through your Asteria Account. Timeclock does when you're signed in with Sync on: your clients, tags and shifts are kept on Asteria's server for your account, sealed (encrypted) before they're stored, and read back only to your devices. Turning Sync off stops it; Delete all data in Timeclock erases the account's copy, and deleting your Asteria Account deletes it.

Apple Intelligence

Keys uses Apple Intelligence through Apple's Foundation Models framework, on your device. Writing Tools, Image Playground and Siri are Apple's, under Apple's privacy policy.

Siri and Spotlight

Where an app offers Siri, Shortcuts, Spotlight or Handoff, sharing is opt-in. The app indexes the content on your device. Siri requests are handled by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy. You can turn sharing off and remove the index from Settings.

Children

Our apps are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If we learn that an Asteria Account belongs to a child under 13, we delete it.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions, complaints, or a correction: Asteria support.
