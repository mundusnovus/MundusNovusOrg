Privacy Policy

Smartz, by Asteria

Effective 10 November 2026. Smartz is the app that used to be called Asteria; Smartz is also the AI in every Asteria app and at smartz.chat.

The short version: your messages are end-to-end encrypted and Asteria Technology can't read them. Smartz and your bots answer through our gateway, which keeps none of what you send, and our AI provider, Anthropic, answers with Claude (Claude Opus 5.5 with SuperSmartz); Anthropic doesn't train on what you send and deletes it within 30 days unless its safety systems flag it. Smartz's voice is ElevenLabs. Smartz learns about you only from chats you choose to harvest, keeps it private and sealed, and shows you all of it to read or delete. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Your Asteria Account

Your Asteria Account is an account on Asteria Post (post.asteria.technology), our server on the AT Protocol network. To make one we keep your username (also your Post handle), your display name, your email address (for account recovery and sign-in codes) and your password (stored by the server as a hash). Your Post profile and posts are public on the AT Protocol network, as they are in any app that uses it. Our message server never receives your password: the app signs in to Asteria Post directly and shows our message server only a short-lived proof. At smartz.chat you can sign in with the same account (it can't be made there); the site checks your password with Asteria Post once and keeps a sign-in cookie for 30 days, not your password.

Messages

Messages, photos, videos, voice messages, files, stickers, reactions, edits, read receipts, group details and profiles are encrypted end to end with Asteria Protocol (post-quantum PQXDH and the Double Ratchet, with sender keys for groups). The keys that open them never leave your devices.

To deliver messages our server keeps: your username and display name (so people can find you by username), your public keys, which account a message is for and from, until it is picked up, and, if you turn on notifications, your device's push token and your notification settings. Encrypted messages are deleted when they're delivered, or after 30 days. Encrypted attachments are deleted after 30 days. We don't log IP addresses with messages, and we keep no record of who you talk to after a message is delivered.

Smartz and your bots

Smartz and the bots you make answer with Smartz, the AI by Asteria. The first time anything is asked, the app asks whether to turn it on, and you can turn it off in You › Smartz. To answer, the app sends what's needed: the agent's instructions, your request, recent messages in the chat it's answering in (including other people's messages in a group where you brought it in or mentioned it), photos you show it, and the results of tools it uses (web pages, your connectors, your chats when you ask it to read them). You can keep any chat out of Smartz's reach in that chat's info.

Requests go from your device to Asteria Technology's gateway (asteria.technology/intelligence, or smartz.chat on the web), which passes them to our AI provider and streams the answer back. The gateway doesn't store or log what's sent or answered, except the chats and memories described below that you choose to keep. It keeps how much your account used each day, under a label that can't be traced back to you, for 40 days.

Our AI provider is Anthropic: Smartz answers with Claude Sonnet 5.5, or Claude Opus 5.5 when you turn on SuperSmartz, through Anthropic's Claude API. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, it may not train its models on what we send it or what it answers. It deletes them within 30 days, except requests its safety systems flag, which it keeps for up to two years to enforce its usage policy, as its privacy center describes. It receives no name, username or account, only a label that can't be traced back to you.

A bot's name, username, one-line description and look are stored on our server so people can find it. A bot answers from its owner's devices: if you message someone's bot, the message is encrypted to its owner, its owner can read it, and to answer, the owner's device sends it to Smartz. The app shows this in every chat with someone else's bot.

Smartz's voice

When you have Smartz read something aloud, use audio mode, or dictate a message, the words to speak and the recording of what you said go through our gateway to ElevenLabs, Inc., which turns text into speech and speech into text, under its privacy policy. ElevenLabs receives no name, username or account with them, and handles them under its own policy, which may keep them for a time. Our gateway doesn't keep the audio or the words. While you speak, Apple's speech recognition shows your words on screen, on your device. Spoken replies are cached on your device so a replay doesn't fetch them again; signing out clears them.

What Smartz learns, and your wiki

Smartz learns about you only when you harvest a chat. Then the chat's text goes to Smartz, which writes down what's worth remembering (memories) and adds to pages about what you've discussed (your wiki). For a chat in the Smartz tab or at smartz.chat, the gateway reads it from your account; for an end-to-end encrypted chat, your device sends its text for that harvest only, and only what's learned is kept. Memories and wiki pages are kept on Asteria's server for your Asteria Account, sealed (encrypted) before they're stored, so every Asteria app and smartz.chat can use them. When you talk to Smartz yourself, the memories that fit your question are added to it; they're never used when someone else sets Smartz off.

You can read, edit and delete every memory and page (What Smartz Knows, and the Wiki tab), and delete all memories at once. Your wiki is private until you publish it: then it's public at smartz.chat/your-username, for anyone with the link, until you turn publishing off.

Unified intelligence is off unless you turn it on (You › Smartz › Learning). With it on, a harvest may also write a general skill, like a good way to plan a trip, which joins a shared library that makes Smartz better for everyone. Skills are written to carry nothing personal, and emails, phone numbers, links and handles are removed before they're stored. A skill is used for everyone only once several different people's harvests have produced it. Turning unified intelligence off stops sharing from then on.

Chats that follow you

The Smartz tab is a chat with Smartz that follows your Asteria Account into every Asteria app (Post, Browse, Box, Food and Mood, Swift: Zero to Hero), smartz.chat and every device. Those chats are kept on Asteria's server for your account, sealed before they're stored, and only read back to you. Delete one anywhere to delete it everywhere. At smartz.chat without signing in, chats stay in your browser and nothing you type is kept on our server. Your chats with people and bots in the Chats tab stay end-to-end encrypted and are never kept this way.

The web

When an agent searches the web, your device sends the search words directly to Bing, DuckDuckGo and Wikipedia (through Asteria Search). When it reads a page, your device loads it in a private WebKit view with no cookies kept. Those sites handle the requests under their own policies. Asteria Technology doesn't receive them.

At smartz.chat a browser can't search or open other sites itself, so when Smartz searches the web or reads a page there, Asteria Technology's gateway does it: it sends the search words to DuckDuckGo, Bing and Wikipedia, opens the page, and passes the results back to the chat. It sends no name, account or address of yours with them, and keeps neither the searches nor the pages.

Connectors

If you connect a service (for example Notion, GitHub or Gmail) through the Model Context Protocol, your device talks to that service directly, and your connectors and their sign-ins are stored in your iCloud Keychain, shared by the Asteria apps on your devices (so a connector added here works in Smartz in every Asteria app). Agents use your connectors only when you ask them something, never when someone else sets them off. The service handles your data under its own policy.

The Smartz Subscription

The app is free. The Smartz Subscription's plans, sold through the App Store, give your Asteria Account more Smartz tokens every day in every Asteria app. Apple handles the payment; Asteria receives the App Store's signed record of the purchase (the plan, its dates and transaction number) to give your account the plan, and deletes it with the account. See the full policy.

iCloud

Your bots, their memories, harvested conversations and pages they read are stored in your iCloud account's private database, which only you can read. Asteria Technology can't see it. You can erase it all in the app (You › Smartz).

No ads, no tracking

Smartz has no advertising, no analytics and no third-party trackers. Nothing sent to Smartz is used for advertising or sold.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to You › Delete Asteria Account. This deletes your Asteria Account and your Asteria Post account (with its posts), your bots' usernames, your chats with Smartz, everything Smartz learned about you (memories, wiki, settings), and everything our message server holds for you. You can also erase what your bots learned from your iCloud at the same time. Signing out erases your chats and keys from that device. Skills already shared through unified intelligence carry nothing of yours and stay in the shared library.

Children

Smartz is not directed to children under 13.
