Smartz

Your Smartz, by Asteria. Talk to it, out loud too. It learns from what you share with it, writes your wiki, and it's the same Smartz in every Asteria app.

Coming to TestFlightChat at smartz.chat

Smartz is the AI in every Asteria app, and this is its home. The first tab is your chat with Smartz, the one that follows your Asteria Account into Post, Browse, Box, Food and Mood, Swift: Zero to Hero and smartz.chat, so you can start anywhere and finish here. Have it read answers aloud, or tap the waveform and just talk. Harvest a chat and Smartz remembers what matters, then writes it into your wiki, which stays private until you publish it. It's still a messenger too: design bots with their own system prompts, give them usernames, and chat with them like anyone else, or bring them into a group. Frontier AI built in, so there are no API keys to paste.

Your Smartz

Talk to it

Every answer has a play button, or turn on Read Answers Aloud and Smartz speaks as it writes. Audio mode is a real conversation: it hears when you've finished, and stops when you talk over it. Its own voice, by ElevenLabs.

It learns, and you see what

Harvest any chat, in any Asteria app, and Smartz remembers what matters. What Smartz Knows shows every memory, to keep, change or forget. It learns nothing you didn't hand it.

Your wiki

As you talk, Smartz writes pages about what you've discussed: trips, projects, people, plans. Read them in the Wiki tab. Private, unless you publish it at smartz.chat/you.

Unified intelligence

Turn it on and your harvests also teach Smartz general skills that make it better for everyone. Never anything personal, and off unless you say so.

smartz.chat

Smartz on the web. Sign in with your Asteria Account and your chats, memories and tokens are all there. Try it without signing in, too.

Best value, no surprises

Every reply shows what it cost in Smartz tokens. Long chats never hit a wall; Smartz summarizes the start and keeps going. You get a heads-up before the day's tokens run low, and an answer that's started always finishes.

Agents, as people

Smartz, your assistant

Always there in your chats, too. Pick its tone or write your own. It reads your chats when you ask (and only then), and can ask your other bots for help.

Bots you design

Describe one in a sentence and Smartz drafts its name, look, username and system prompt. Then make it yours: tone, creativity, skills, connectors, and whether strangers can message it.

Usernames for bots

Give a bot a username like @chefbot and anyone can wire it. It answers from your device. Bot usernames always end in “bot”, so nobody mistakes one for a person.

Groups, queue and auto mode

Add bots to a group by username. Bring agents in to answer once, in the order you pick, or put one on auto mode to answer new messages for fifteen minutes, an hour, or a week.

Bots that learn

Memories

Each bot keeps its own memories in your iCloud: facts, preferences, people, plans, know-how, and how you like answers. Smartz can read them all. Teach any bot something by hand, pin what matters, forget what doesn't.

Harvest

One button on any chat. Smartz reads it, writes a summary, and teaches every agent in the chat what's worth knowing: your bots in your iCloud, Smartz on your Asteria Account. A copy of the conversation is saved to your iCloud, so a lost phone loses nothing.

A cache that remembers

Pages your agents read are kept, so they can recall them later without fetching them again. The more you use them, the less you have to repeat yourself.

Tools, not just talk

The web, keyless

Agents search with Asteria Search (Bing, DuckDuckGo, Wikipedia and news, ads removed) and read pages in a private, invisible WebKit browser. Fresh information, in a group or one to one, with no API key anywhere.

Documents and spreadsheets

Ask for a report, a letter, a plan, a budget or a tracker. Agents write real Word, PDF, Markdown, Excel and CSV files and attach them to the chat.

Connectors

Model Context Protocol, built in. A directory of more than forty hosted servers, checked live: Notion, Linear, Jira and Confluence, GitHub, Stripe, Figma, Canva, Gmail, Google Calendar and Drive, DeepWiki, Context7, Hugging Face, Exa and more. Sign in once with OAuth, or add any server by its URL.

Trust, built in

Connectors use your accounts, so agents use them only when you ask. When someone else sets an agent off (a mention, auto mode, a chat with your bot) it gets the web, documents and its own know-how, and nothing private.

One Asteria Account

Your Asteria Account is the one account for every Asteria app that needs one, and it is also your Asteria Post account: the same username, the same password, one sign-up from either app. No invite codes. Make it in Smartz and Post signs you in by itself, and so does smartz.chat.

Private by design

End-to-end encrypted

Messages, photos, files, profiles and everything agents say in chats with people are sealed with Asteria Protocol: post-quantum PQXDH and the Double Ratchet, with sender keys for groups. Our server can't read any of it.

Yours, and plain about it

Chats with your own bots are kept on your iPhone. To answer, what an agent reads goes through our gateway, which keeps none of it, to our AI provider in the US, which keeps nothing either; the app asks before the first time. What Smartz learns is sealed on your account and yours to read or delete. Your bots answer from your devices, which is why a bot's owner can read what people say to it, and the app tells them so.

No ads. Ever.

No advertising, no analytics, no trackers. The server forgets each message once it's delivered.
