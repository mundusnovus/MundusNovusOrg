Answers to common questions. Still stuck? Email us and a person will help.

Your account

Do I need an invite?

No. Download Smartz and make an Asteria Account: a name, a username, your email and a password.

Is my Asteria Account the same as my Asteria Post account?

Yes. One account, one username, one password. Sign up in either app and the other works too. If you already have an Asteria Post account, sign in to Smartz with it. At smartz.chat you sign in with the same account; it can't be made on the web.

How do I delete my account?

You › Delete Asteria Account, then type your password. It deletes your Asteria Account, your Asteria Post account and your bots' usernames.

Smartz's voice

How do I hear an answer?

Tap the speaker under any answer, or turn on Read Answers Aloud in the ⋯ menu and Smartz speaks as it writes. Pick its voice in You › Smartz › Voice; Smartz's own voice is the first.

How does audio mode work?

Tap the waveform beside the message field. Talk, then pause, and Smartz answers out loud. Talk over it to interrupt, or tap the hand. It works best with headphones or in a quiet room.

What does voice cost?

Reading an answer aloud is about 1 Smartz token per 250 characters (twice that with the expressive voice); talking to Smartz is under a token a minute. Answers you've already heard replay for free.

What Smartz learns

How does Smartz learn about me?

Only when you harvest a chat (⋯ › Harvest This Chat, in any Asteria app). Smartz remembers what matters and adds to your wiki. See and delete all of it in What Smartz Knows.

How do I publish my wiki?

Wiki tab › Publish at smartz.chat. Anyone with the link can read it at smartz.chat/your-username until you turn it off.

What is unified intelligence?

An opt-in: your harvests also teach Smartz general skills that help everyone, never anything personal. Turn it on or off in You › Smartz › Learning.

Smartz and your bots

Why can't I use agents?

Agents answer with Smartz, so they need an internet connection and your OK. If you tapped Not Now, turn it on in You › Smartz. If you've used today's Smartz tokens, they refresh at midnight UTC.

Where do my questions to agents go?

To Smartz, through our gateway, which keeps none of it, and on to Gemini 4 Argon, Google's AI model; Google doesn't train on it and keeps it for up to 55 days only to check for abuse. The privacy policy has the details.

How do I make a bot?

Tap + New bot above your chats (or in Agents). Describe it in a sentence and tap Design it with Smartz, or fill it in yourself. Give it a username ending in “bot” if you want other people to be able to message it.

How do I add a bot to a group?

Open the group, tap its name, then Add a bot, and type its username. A bot runs on its owner's devices, so it can join groups its owner is in.

What are queue and auto mode?

In any chat, tap ⋯ › Bring in an agent. Pick agents to answer once, in order, or turn on auto mode so one answers new messages by itself for the time you choose.

Why didn't my bot answer someone?

Bots answer from your device, so Smartz has to be open (or recently open) on your iPhone or iPad. Check that the bot has “Anyone can message it” on.

What does Harvest do?

It saves the conversation to your iCloud and teaches the agents in it what's worth remembering. Open a chat, tap ⋯, then Harvest. See everything they've learned in Agents › Memories.

Connectors

How do I connect Notion, GitHub or another service?

Agents › Connectors. Open servers work straight away; others sign you in with the service. You can also add any MCP server by its URL.

Why does Gmail ask for a client ID?

Google's Workspace MCP servers are in Developer Preview and don't let apps register themselves. Create an OAuth client of type iOS in Google Cloud with the bundle ID technology.asteria.wire and paste its client ID.

Privacy and safety

Can Asteria Technology read my messages?

No. They're end-to-end encrypted with Asteria Protocol. Only the people (and, for bots, the bot's owner) in a chat can read it.

What's a safety number?

Each one-to-one chat with a person has a 60-digit safety number. Compare it in person or on a call. If yours match, no one is in the middle. Open the chat, tap the name, then Safety number.

How do I block someone?

Open the chat, tap the name, and tap Block.
