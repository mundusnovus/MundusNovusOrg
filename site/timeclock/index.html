Asteria Timeclock

Hours, breaks, pay. Yours. Not a dashboard’s. Not ours. Yours.

A timekeeping suite for hourly freelancers who would rather work than maintain a spreadsheet. Punch in, take a break, clock out, and get a timesheet that looks like someone cared. It has no Asteria account and no Asteria server; your shifts live in your own private iCloud.

What it does

Punch, break, clock out

A live dial shows the shift as it happens. Start a paid or unpaid break with one tap. On clock-out, review the end time, breaks and note before the shift is saved.

Fix the day you forgot

Add a backdated shift, duplicate last week’s, split a long day, edit any time, or delete a shift and restore it from Recently Deleted for 30 days. Life happens; the timesheet should survive it.

Clients, rates and overtime

Give each client a rate, currency, pay period, overtime rules, rounding rules and a colour. Each shift keeps the rate it was worked at, so a raise never rewrites history.

A timesheet, not a project

Export payroll-ready CSV, or a clean Asteria-branded PDF with the period, client, breaks, hours, rate, earnings and totals. Choose the range and filter by client or tag.

Siri, widgets, Live Activity

Clock in, clock out, start a break or ask how many hours. Widgets and the Live Activity keep the timer on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. The Mac menu bar does the same without opening a window.

Apple Intelligence, on device

Describe a shift in plain words and get a draft you confirm. Ask for a short summary of a period, written from the numbers already on screen. The model never changes pay, and nothing is saved without your confirmation.

The private bit

No account. No tracking. No analytics. No ads. Your shifts are in your private iCloud database; exports are files you choose to make. Siri and Spotlight sharing are opt-in. Calendar access is read-only. You can delete everything from Settings, which is more than most time-tracking software lets you do without an email to support.
