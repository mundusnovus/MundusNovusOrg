Asteria Timeclock · Support

The clock is wrong. Obviously.

Email me@demetria.xyz with the app version, the device, the client or shift involved, and what the timesheet should say. A screenshot of the review sheet is more useful than a description of your feelings about it.

Common ones

A shift is still running

Open Today and tap Clock Out. If you missed the end time, tap the running shift, choose Edit, and set the end. The live Activity and widgets are shortcuts, not a second copy of the truth; the shift record wins.

A client rate changed

Old shifts keep the rate they were worked at. That is deliberate: a raise should not rewrite last month’s invoice. Edit the shift itself if the historical rate was wrong.

The PDF total looks wrong

Check the date range, the client filter and whether breaks are paid or unpaid. The PDF shows the same rules as the timesheet; if the rule is wrong, fix the client or the shift, not the PDF renderer.

iCloud is not syncing

Settings says whether the app is using iCloud or this device. Sign in to iCloud in System Settings, relaunch, and give the first sync a minute. A shift that was running on the Watch should appear on the iPhone once CloudKit catches up. It is not instantaneous, despite what the marketing copy implies.

Siri does not know the app

Open Settings → Siri and Shortcuts, make sure sharing is on, then try the exact phrase with the app name: “Clock in with Asteria Timeclock.” Siri is literal. It is not being difficult on purpose, which is somehow worse.

Privacy

Read the privacy policy. Shifts are stored in your private iCloud database; exports are files you choose to create. There is no Asteria account and no Asteria server.
