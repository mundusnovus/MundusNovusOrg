The license

The open-source projects listed here use the Asteria License 1.0. This is not a blanket license for all Asteria products. Its legal terms are the GNU General Public License, version 3, unchanged, so it works with any GPLv3 project. Use GPL-3.0-only in package metadata.

Pictures, songs and documents you make with our tools are yours. Using the apps or the Asteria Pixel typeface does not put your work under the GPL.

The license opens with a short statement of why we share: every person has a dignity no price can measure, good tools belong in every hand, and AI should serve people rather than replace their judgement. It is our conviction, not a condition. You need not share our faith to use, study, change, sell or share this work.

Other people's work inside ours keeps its own license: BeepBox (MIT) and lamejs (LGPL) in Asteria Box.

Your work and your privacy

  • We will never include ads in any Asteria app. Accounts, storage and privacy details vary by product; Social and our connected web services use an Asteria account.
  • Your drawings and songs stay on your device until you save or share them.
  • The assistants in the hosted Sprite and Box editors use your own Anthropic key. The key is kept only on your device and is sent only to Anthropic, with your requests, when you use the assistant.
  • The hosted Sprite and Box editors remain available at no cost. See the Asteria privacy policy and the support page for the native apps.

Make it with us

For an open-source project, found a problem or have an idea? Open an issue or a pull request in its repository. When you send a change, you offer it under these same terms and keep your copyright.
