Before you email

Most “iCloud is not syncing” reports are the same three things: the device is not signed in to iCloud, the app was built without the entitlement, or Settings says it is on this device only. Open Settings in the app and read the status before writing the email. It will save us both a round trip.

For Asteria Intelligence (Asteria, Post, Browse and Food and Mood), check you’re online and that it’s on in the app’s Settings. If it says you’ve used today’s Asteria Intelligence, it refreshes at midnight UTC. For Apple Intelligence (Keys, Timeclock and Swift: Zero to Hero), check that the device supports it and that it is turned on in System Settings. Either way, the app will tell you when it is unavailable. It is not being coy.
