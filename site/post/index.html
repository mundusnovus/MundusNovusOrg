Asteria Post

Social, minus the shouting. Your feed, by Asteria Intelligence.

Post is a calmer, smarter social app on the open AT Protocol network, home of Asteria Post and Bluesky. Asteria Intelligence reads what's new and puts thoughtful posts first. Outrage and promotion sink. There are no ads, and when you reach the end, Post tells you you're all caught up. We're told this is unusual.

Join the beta on TestFlightpost.asteria.technology

Screens show sample posts. Asteria Intelligence asks before it turns on, and you can switch it off in Settings.

What it does

A feed made by Asteria Intelligence

Asteria Intelligence describes what's new and a tested ranker on your device puts thoughtful posts first. Promotion always sinks. Virality is dampened. The model never decides alone.

An algorithm you control

Turn dials for calm, freshness, people you follow, and media. Hide topics, mute words, and see exactly how it works. Every post in For You can explain why it's there.

Ask for a feed, for as long as you want

Say “space and cooking, nothing political, for two hours”. Post shows what it understood, builds that feed, and goes back to normal on its own when the time is up.

Ask about any post

Get the context, the gist of the replies, or a plain explanation, from Asteria Intelligence. Or tap Catch Me Up and read the summary instead of the scroll.

Reels

Videos up to three minutes, full screen, one swipe at a time. Double-tap to like, hold for 2×, scrub, and reply without leaving the video.

Share in full resolution

Photos and videos up to 250 MB. Asteria Intelligence can suggest descriptions for your photos, so everyone can enjoy them.

Messages

Chat with anyone on Asteria Post and Bluesky, react with emoji, and share posts. You choose who can message you. Messages aren't end-to-end encrypted, and the app says so.

Your profile, your way

Photo, banner, bio, pronouns, website, a pinned post, and a QR card to share. Notifications for replies, mentions, quotes, follows, likes and reposts, each one switchable.

Safe by design

Report, block and mute from any post. Asteria Post checks new posts for scams, phishing and malware. An optional reminder suggests a break, which is more than most feeds will.

Your home on the open network

Join Asteria Post from the app and get a .post handle of your own: you.post. Elsewhere on the network it reads in full, as you.post.asteria.technology. Follow anyone on Bluesky and the wider AT Protocol network, or sign in with the Bluesky account you already have. Your posts and followers are yours, on an open protocol, not ours to hold hostage.

Siri, with a job

“Catch me up on Post” reads you a summary. Siri can also start a themed feed, end it, and post for you. It still won't write your hot takes. That's a feature.

Our promise

Post is free, with no in-app purchases, because there's nothing in it we could honestly sell you. No ads, ever. Coming soon to the App Store for iPhone, iPad and Mac. Until then, the beta is open on TestFlight.
