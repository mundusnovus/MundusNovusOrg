Asteria Browse

Asteria Browse is a calm browser for Mac, iPhone and iPad, with Spaces, a sidebar of favorites, pinned tabs and tabs, and search that runs on your device with no account.

Search

Typing in the address bar searches with Asteria Search. Your device asks Wikipedia, DuckDuckGo and Bing directly and merges the results. If a source asks to check that you're a person, Asteria skips it and shows the others.

Spaces and tabs

Keep separate Spaces for work, study and play. Pin a tab or make it a favorite and it remembers its home page; choose Reset to Saved Home to go back to it.

Private tabs

A private tab keeps cookies and site data in a temporary store, is left out of history, and saves downloads to a temporary folder. It does not hide your traffic from websites or your network.

Downloads

On Mac, downloads go to your Downloads folder. On iPhone and iPad they are in the Files app, under On My iPhone or On My iPad › Asteria Browse.
